# Finance & Cash Collection Analysis

## Project Overview
This project evaluates the financial performance of a retail and distribution business, focusing on revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, payment collection, refunds, outstanding payments, and departmental payroll budgets.

## Analysis Performed
- Calculated revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, and gross margin.
- Analysed completed, pending, and refunded payments.
- Examined revenue-to-payment differences.
- Evaluated departmental payroll against available budget figures.
- Compared profitability across product categories.

## Key Findings
- The difference between recognised order revenue and completed payments is approximately $275,848.
- Refunded payments total approximately $144,618.
- Pending payments total approximately $131,230.
- Five of the seven departments have payroll above their stated budget figures.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PostgreSQL
- Python (OpenPyXL) — Excel workbook automation and preparation.

## Business Recommendations
Strengthen payment reconciliation, improve monitoring of outstanding balances, investigate refund patterns, and review departmental payroll variances.

## Data Note
The analysis uses a synthetic practice dataset. Financial findings are based on the fields and assumptions available in the source data.
